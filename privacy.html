Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Game Checklist is a desktop app made by an independent developer (contact: gamechecklist.dev@gmail.com). This page explains what the app stores, where, and why.

On your computer

Your games, tasks, trophies, settings and proof pictures and clips are stored on your own computer. Proof pictures and clips never leave your computer.

Online features (only when you play online)

The online features use Google Firebase (servers in the European Union). When you play online, the app creates an account for you without asking for an email. It stores:

  • Your player card: player name and number (like Name#1234), avatar, level, achievement points, trophy and badge counts, title and look, status and status message, the game you are playing and up to 16 game titles. Other signed-in players can see a card when they know your name and number.
  • Friends: your friend list, friend requests and the players you blocked.
  • Chat messages between you and a friend. Only the two of you can read them. Rude words are hidden in the app.
  • Reports you send about another player. Only the developer can read them.

Link with Google and the online save

If you choose “Link with Google”, you sign in on Google’s own page in your browser. The app receives your Google account’s id, name and email address; it never sees your Google password. With a linked account, a copy of your progress (games, tasks, trophies and settings, but no proof pictures or clips) is saved online so you can load it on another PC. Only you can read your online save.

Feedback

When you send feedback from the app, your note, an optional screenshot you attach, the app version, the language, your player name and a short device line (for example “Desktop app · Windows · 1920×1080”) are posted to the developer’s private Discord channel.

Game information

To find games and their details, the app sends your search words to Wikidata and Wikipedia. Pictures of games load from Steam and Wikimedia. These services see your internet address like any website does.

What the app does not do

  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics and no selling of data.
  • No access to your files beyond what you choose to open in the app.

How long data is kept, and your choices

Online data is kept while you use the online features. You can play fully offline at any time. To get a copy of your online data, or to have your player card, friends, messages and online save deleted, email gamechecklist.dev@gmail.com with your player name and number. If you live in the EU, you have the rights of the GDPR, including access, correction, deletion and complaint to your data protection authority.

Children

The online features are meant for players aged 13 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
